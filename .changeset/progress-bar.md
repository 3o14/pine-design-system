Add `ProgressBar` component built on `@base-ui/react` Progress. Supports determinate and indeterminate (`value={null}`) states, three sizes, and six color intents, with theming for basic, game, and crayon. Renders `role="progressbar"` with full ARIA wiring — `label` is required for an accessible name and `formatValue` drives both the displayed value and `aria-valuetext`.
